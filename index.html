Motivation Letter

Dear Members of the Selection Committee,

I am writing to express my strong motivation to pursue advanced study in Computer Science, with a particular focus on Artificial Intelligence, Deep Learning, Computer Vision, and Explainable AI. I recently completed my Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from North Bengal International University, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.71/4.00. Throughout my undergraduate studies, I developed a strong interest in artificial intelligence, which gradually evolved into a clear research-oriented career goal.

My academic journey has been shaped significantly by research. My work has focused on developing deep learning models for real-world problems, particularly in medical image analysis and satellite image classification. My final-year thesis, titled “Adaptive Failure-Aware Segmentation Framework (AFA-Seg)”, explored a robust medical image segmentation framework integrating ConvNeXt-Transformer architecture, adaptive failure detection, retraining strategies, ensemble learning, and explainable AI techniques. Through this work, I gained practical experience in designing experiments, evaluating deep learning models, analyzing failure cases, and developing solutions to improve model reliability.

In addition to my thesis, I have conducted research on satellite image classification using hybrid deep learning architectures. One of my research works was published in IEEE, while another paper, “Enhancing Multi-Class Satellite Image Classification with MRCL-ELM: A Hybrid Explainable Deep Learning Approach,” is published in Neural Computing and Applications. This research combined EfficientNet, CNN, LSTM, and Extreme Learning Machine techniques and incorporated LIME and SHAP to improve model interpretability. These experiences strengthened my understanding of both the technical and research aspects of artificial intelligence and confirmed my desire to pursue research at a higher level.

I am particularly interested in advancing my knowledge of deep learning, computer vision, medical image analysis, remote sensing, Transformer-based architectures, and explainable AI. I want to investigate how intelligent systems can become not only more accurate but also more robust, interpretable, and applicable to real-world challenges. In the long term, I aspire to become an AI researcher and contribute to research in areas such as autonomous systems, medical imaging, and intelligent analysis of Earth observation data.

I believe that joining your academic environment would provide me with the opportunity to strengthen my theoretical foundation, develop advanced research skills, collaborate with researchers, and contribute meaningfully to ongoing research. I am prepared to work diligently, learn from challenges, and actively contribute to the academic and research community.

This opportunity represents an important step toward my long-term goal of becoming an independent researcher in Artificial Intelligence. I would be honored to pursue my studies in an environment that values rigorous research, innovation, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Thank you for considering my application. I sincerely hope to have the opportunity to contribute to and grow within your academic community.

Sincerely,
Md. Ashik Ahmmed
Bangladesh
